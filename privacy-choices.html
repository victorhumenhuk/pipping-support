Privacy choices

Pipping keeps the core product local to the device. The app does not provide a Pipping account or cloud-sync switch in this release.

Inside Pipping, a person can delete an individual private journal photo. To remove all local Pipping data, delete the app from the device, subject to Apple's device backup and restoration behaviour.

For help with a privacy control or a data-protection request, email pipping@thesmios.com. Do not include sensitive details unless they are necessary to resolve the request.
