Privacy notice

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Pipping is provided by Thesmios. For privacy questions or data-rights requests, email pipping@thesmios.com.

Information Pipping stores

Pipping stores the profile, goals, urge check-ins, rescue activity, Pip Plan answers and preferences that a person chooses to add. These can be sensitive personal information.

This release stores that information in local app storage on the person's device. It has no Pipping account, CloudKit sync, social pairing service or Pipping server for those entries.

If a person chooses a private nail-journal photo, Pipping stores a compressed copy in local app storage. The journal marks its photo files to stay out of device backups. Pipping does not use journal photos in widgets, notifications, sharing or companion artwork.

Why Pipping uses information

Pipping uses local entries to provide the app, remember a person's chosen goal, show local progress and keep their personal plan together. Pipping does not use an advertising identifier, sell habit data, show ads or use behavioral analytics in this release.

Apple features a person chooses

Apple handles App Store purchases and restoration through StoreKit. Pipping does not receive payment-card details.

Pipping asks before it can send reminders. It opens the photo picker or camera only when a person chooses to add a private journal photo. Apple and the device settings govern those permissions.

Controls, deletion and contact

A person can delete a journal photo inside Pipping. Pipping has no server copy of the person's local entries. A person can remove the app and its local data from their device, subject to Apple's device backup and restoration behavior.

Feedback emails contain only what the person chooses to send. Pipping never adds private habit entries, plans or photos automatically.

If Pipping's data practices change, this notice and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed practice is released.
