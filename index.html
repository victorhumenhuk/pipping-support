Support

Pipping is a gentle, self-guided companion for noticing nail-biting urges, trying a small hand-based action and learning what helps. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or promise a health result.

For support, accessibility feedback or a data-protection question, email pipping@thesmios.com.

Do not include private notes, urge entries, Pip Plan answers or journal photos unless they are necessary for the question. Pipping never attaches them to an email automatically.

If someone has bleeding, infection signs, persistent pain, significant distress, self-harm concerns or a safeguarding emergency, they should contact a trusted adult, local medical care or emergency services. Pipping is not emergency care.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your device. This release has no Pipping account, no cloud sync and no Pipping server for your entries.

What is Pip Plan? An optional one-time App Store purchase. It never renews. Apple handles payment and applicable refunds.

How do I delete my data? Delete a journal photo inside the app, or remove the app to remove its local data, subject to Apple's device backup behaviour.
