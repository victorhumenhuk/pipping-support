Terms and safety

Effective date: 22 August 2026

Pipping is self-guided educational support for nail-biting patterns. It offers brief actions, reflection and a personal plan. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, promise an outcome or replace a qualified professional.

If someone has bleeding, infection signs, persistent pain, significant distress, self-harm concerns or a safeguarding emergency, they should contact a trusted adult, local medical care or emergency services.

Pip Plan is an optional non-consumable App Store purchase. It is a one-time purchase and does not renew as a subscription. Apple shows the current price, taxes and purchase terms before payment. Apple handles payment and applicable refunds.

If a person is below the age of digital consent where they live, they should ask a parent or guardian before making a purchase, adding a photo or opening an outside support link.

Pipping can link to external services or open an email application. Those services are independent and have their own terms and privacy information.
